    About this extension

    This tool helps Filipino creators in our thesis study keep a copy of their own TikTok Studio analytics as CSV files. It reads the same numbers you can already see in TikTok Studio and saves them locally — nothing more.

    It produces two files: video performance and follower history.

    Your data & privacy

    Runs entirely on your computer. The files are saved to your Downloads folder. The extension never sends your data to us or anyone else.

    You choose what to share. Only the files you decide to send your researcher ever leave your device.

    Consent & withdrawal

    You agreed to take part in this study by signing the informed consent form. Using this tool is voluntary.

    You may withdraw at any time and ask that your shared files be deleted — no reason needed.
